How it works

The lifecycle is the control.

01

Request

A caller presents one concrete action with its five-part context.

02

Authorize

The guard captures an immutable snapshot and deterministic fingerprint.

03

Compare

At execution time, every approved field is compared with current reality.

04

Allow or block

An exact match runs. Any drift blocks and requires a new authorization.
